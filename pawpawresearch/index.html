Welcome to the Pawpaw Research Alliance

The Pawpaw Research Alliance is a fictitious nonprofit organization dedicated to the scientific study, conservation, and promotion of the North American pawpaw (Asimina triloba). Our mission is to expand knowledge of this unique native fruit tree, support sustainable cultivation, and preserve wild populations for future generations.

Why Study the Pawpaw?

The American pawpaw produces the largest edible fruit native to the United States. Its creamy, custard-like flesh offers a flavor often described as a blend of banana, mango, and melon. Once a staple of Indigenous diets and early settlers, the species has declined in some regions due to habitat loss and limited commercial interest.

Our Core Goals

  • Conduct field and laboratory research on genetics, pollination, and pest resistance
  • Document and conserve genetic diversity of wild pawpaw populations
  • Develop improved cultivars suited to different climate zones
  • Educate growers, chefs, and the public about this underappreciated native fruit

Explore our pages to learn about ongoing projects, the biology of Asimina triloba, practical cultivation advice, and ways to get involved. Together we can help this remarkable tree reclaim its place in North American landscapes and kitchens.
