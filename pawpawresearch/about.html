About the Pawpaw Research Alliance

Founded in 2018 as a collaborative effort among botanists, horticulturists, and native plant enthusiasts, the Pawpaw Research Alliance (PRA) works to advance scientific understanding of Asimina triloba and related species. Although this website represents a fictitious organization created for educational portfolio purposes, its content reflects real botanical knowledge and genuine research needs surrounding the North American pawpaw.

Our History

Interest in the pawpaw has grown steadily as people rediscover native foods and seek climate-resilient crops. Early Alliance members recognized that commercial success and long-term conservation both require solid data on genetics, pollination biology, disease resistance, and post-harvest handling. From modest beginnings with volunteer field surveys, the Alliance has expanded into multi-state monitoring programs and partnerships with university research stations.

What We Value

  • Scientific rigor — All projects follow transparent methods and share data openly when possible.
  • Conservation first — We prioritize protecting wild genetic resources before introducing new plantings.
  • Community engagement — Growers, foragers, Indigenous knowledge holders, and students are essential partners.
  • Practical outcomes — Research must translate into better cultivars, better growing guides, and better market opportunities.

Leadership Team (Fictitious)

Our current board includes specialists in plant genetics, entomology, and sustainable agriculture. Day-to-day operations are coordinated by a small staff based in the eastern United States, with regional volunteers collecting phenology data and seed samples across the natural range of the species.

The Alliance is committed to ethical research practices, including respect for Indigenous cultural connections to the pawpaw and careful consideration of how wild populations are sampled.
