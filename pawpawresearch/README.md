Pawpaw Research Alliance

This is a multi-page educational website dedicated to introducing the North American pawpaw (Asimina triloba), the largest edible fruit native to the United States.

The pawpaw is a distinctive understory tree that produces large, soft, custard-like fruits with a unique flavor often compared to a blend of banana, mango, and melon. Although the plant was once well known and valued by Indigenous peoples and early European settlers, it has become relatively obscure in modern times. Many people today have never tasted or even seen a fresh pawpaw. This website was created to help change that by making accurate and accessible information about the species available to a wider audience.

The primary purpose of the site is to popularize knowledge about the North American pawpaw. It presents clear explanations of the plant’s biology, natural habitat, ecological role, cultivation requirements, and scientific interest. Topics covered include the structure and appearance of the tree, its distinctive maroon flowers, the characteristics of the fruit and seeds, preferred growing conditions, pollination challenges, and selected named cultivars. The content is written in straightforward language suitable for students, home gardeners, educators, and anyone curious about native plants and underutilized fruits.

Please note that the Pawpaw Research Alliance is a completely fictitious organization. It does not exist in the real world and was invented solely for educational and portfolio demonstration purposes. While the botanical and horticultural information presented throughout the website is based on established scientific knowledge of Asimina triloba, the organization, its research projects, staff, and contact details are entirely fictional.

The website consists of six interconnected pages designed to guide visitors through different aspects of the topic:

Home – A general introduction to the pawpaw and the purpose of the site
About – Background information about the fictional organization and its imaginary goals
Research – An overview of key research themes related to the species, such as genetics, pollination, and conservation
Biology – Detailed descriptions of the plant’s morphology, habitat, range, and ecological relationships
Cultivation – Practical information on how to grow pawpaw trees, including site selection, pollination tips, and recommended varieties
Contact – Illustrative contact details and links to real external resources for further reading

All pages share a consistent layout and navigation structure. The site uses an external stylesheet and includes images of actual pawpaw trees, flowers, and fruits sourced from public-domain collections.

To explore the website, open the index.html file in any modern web browser. No special software or installation is required.
